# 🐾 Golden Biscuit

> *A 7-level 2D side-scrolling adventure — sunrise to neon night — starring Biscuit, the world's most determined Jack Russell terrier.*

[![Play Now](https://img.shields.io/badge/🎮%20Play%20Now-Live%20Site-FFD700?style=for-the-badge)](https://rastillman-hash.github.io/Golden-Biscuit/)

## The Story

Biscuit's family packed their bags and headed to Wonderland Park for the weekend — dropping him off at **Happy Paws Day Care** without a second thought. Big mistake.

Biscuit is a highly intelligent senior Jack Russell terrier with places to be and a peanut butter biscuit with his name on it. He's breaking out, crossing the city, and catching up with his family — one level at a time.

---

## Objective

Navigate **7 levels** from a Doggy Day Care at sunrise all the way to a theme park under neon lights. Each level ends with a boss fight. Defeat the boss, reach the exit, and carry Biscuit home.

Complete all 7 levels to earn the **Golden Peanut Butter Biscuit** — then replay the entire game with every upgrade unlocked from the start for the ultimate Golden Biscuit run.

## Gameplay

### Health & Food
Biscuit starts each level at full health. Enemies and hazards deal damage — the further into the game, the harder they hit. Collect food scattered throughout the level to heal up.

| Food | Heal |
|---|---|
| Kibble | +5 hp |
| Treat | +15 hp |
| Biscuit | +25 hp |
| Hotdog | +35 hp |
| Burger | +50 hp |

If Biscuit reaches 0 health, you restart from the last **checkpoint**. Checkpoints activate automatically when you run through them.

### Scoring
- Defeat enemies to earn points
- Collect food for bonus points
- Earn a level-completion bonus at the end of each stage
- Your top 10 scores are saved locally — enter 3-character initials to claim your spot

### High Scores
There are **two separate leaderboards** — one for a normal first run and one for the **Golden Biscuit run**. Golden run scores get their own glittering board.

## Upgrades

After completing each level you choose **one upgrade** to carry forward. Upgrades stack and persist until the end of the game.

| Upgrade | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 💨 **Sprint Burst** | `Shift` | Unleashes a short speed burst with a cooldown timer |
| 🦘 **Double Jump** | `Space` × 2 | Jump a second time while airborne |
| ⚙️ **Power Drill** | `↓` | Dig through terrain and obstacles that normally block you |
| 🦷 **Big Chompers** | `E` | Extended bite range — chew through metal and tougher enemies |

All four upgrades are available by the start of **Level 6**. Beat Level 7 and they all unlock permanently for the Golden Biscuit replay.
